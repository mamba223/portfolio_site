THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

I am a software engineer pursuing a Master of Science in Computer Science, with an academic background in both computer science and economics. I enjoy designing practical systems that connect strong software engineering fundamentals with emerging AI and data technologies.

My work covers API design, distributed services, databases, cloud deployment, information retrieval, and full-stack development. I think beyond whether a feature works: I consider data integrity, security, observability, testing, performance, and how a system can be maintained or extended as its requirements change.

Recent work includes a Django-based report management platform used by approximately 50 people, a conversation-aware retrieval-augmented chatbot, and experiments in reactive robotics and distributed systems. I have also developed ReelForge, an episodic AI video platform; Collabo, a local-first collaborative workspace; and Lumina, a desktop AI voice assistant.

My economics background adds a complementary perspective: I am interested not only in how technology is built, but also in the decisions, workflows, and measurable outcomes it enables. I am especially drawn to engineering roles where I can own challenging problems, learn quickly, and contribute to useful, dependable products.
